# Learn an effect by leaving the rule out, safely

Do: "Followed; effect unknown" means every step had the rule, so nothing can be compared. Two ways to learn: propose turning on withholding — a small seeded share of steps runs without one rule that may be withheld, never a safety rule — or replay a recorded step without the facet and compare the checks. Quote effects with both group sizes; fewer than five on either side is not a result.

Check: Your report states, for each effect it quotes, the number of steps with and without the rule.

Stop: Withholding at 10 percent of steps or less, 1 rule per step. Turning it on is the user's decision: it trades a little quality now for knowledge.

Why: Without a comparison, every rule looks necessary and none can ever be removed.
