### Added
- **Criteria and their source in the domains** (plan 2.8, domain adapters). arch-flow 0.3.5 and viz-flow 0.4.5 accept a `criteria` block in the brief's Acceptance, held to the same gate as the core's. `[look: …]` counts as judged by a person, like `[user]` (the owner's decision of 22.11), and at most three such criteria are allowed — in the block and in the numbered lines. `brief approve` records the list and its hash, which equals the core's `criteriaHash`. `facet domain import` turns that record into the item's `criterion` event. Both `domain.json` files declare `chains`: links with the step that produces each, and how each pair is compared. In arch, `spec>model` is compared by `spec_compare.py` (exit 0 the same, 1 differs, 2 cannot say); a drawing or task read by eye is `human`. In viz, no tool compares the links yet. `facet origin` on a domain item follows that chain; commands name `{python}`, `{scripts}`, `{work}`, `{item}` and `{criterion}`.
