### Added
- **The source step of a finding** (plan 2.8, steps 3–6). `facet origin --criterion A3 --from return|human|defect` — and `facet flow verdict REWORK … --criterion A3` on its own — records an `origin` event. A criterion not in the brief goes to the brief, and nobody who worked is charged. Otherwise the first differing pair on the criterion's `chain` is the source (`chain`). Otherwise the criterion's check is run on the snapshots after each step from `by` to where the problem was found: never met → `not-done`; broken later → bisection finds the step (`measured`). Else it is the step the reviewer names (`judged`). The trust graph charges from origin events: the source 1, each judge in between 0.5, a false green, or the tool for `--tool-defect`. The board shows K1–K5.
