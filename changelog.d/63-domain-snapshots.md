### Added
- **Domain findings are measured on the states of the steps** (plan 2.8, the rest). arch-flow and viz-flow copy the item's JSON files to `work/<ITEM>/snap/<step>/` when a step starts. `measure` in `domain.json` says how a criterion's check is answered on such a state, through `check_one.py` (shared by both domains). In arch, the tool runs again when the state holds its inputs (`spec_compare.py`, `model_spec.py`); otherwise the state's results files are read, and only one with a tool's receipt counts. With a threshold, the criterion's own threshold decides. `facet origin` bisects these states for a domain item as it does git snapshots in the core: `measured`, `regression` or `not-done`. A state that cannot say leaves the finding `judged`.
