# Choose the model by the kind of work

Do: Sort each step before assigning it. Mechanical — search, run, compare, fill a template: the smallest model. Implementation against a written contract: the middle one. Design, ambiguity, a refactor across modules: the largest. Escalate one step only on a concrete trigger: two failed attempts, contradictory evidence, or a decision the handoffs do not contain. Go back down afterwards. Write model and trigger into the work order.

Check: The order names a model per phase, and every escalation has its trigger next to it.

Stop: 1 escalation per phase. Needing a second means the task is under-specified: return it to whoever wrote the contract.

Why: Most steps of agent work are mechanical; the top price for a search buys nothing.
